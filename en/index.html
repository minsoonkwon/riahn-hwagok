About

Near Hwagok Station Exit 7,
RIAHN Hair Hwagok Station

RIAHN Hair Hwagok Station offers a wide range of hair services, from cuts, perms and color to scalp treatments. We run a separate styling section just for men, offering tailored cuts, perms and color.

We plan your next visit around how long styles last — 8–12 weeks for perms and 4–12 weeks for color — and recommend using home-care products. We also offer discounts for Naver Booking and photo reviews.

We are at Unit 201, 2F, 135 Hwagok-ro, Gangseo-gu, Seoul. We are 298 m from Exit 7 of Hwagok Station on Subway Line 5. Come out of Exit 7 and walk straight along the street to our building. Opened in 2025 with 10 styling chairs, we are open daily 10:00–20:00.

Why RIAHN Hair Hwagok Station

  • A separate styling section just for men.
  • Planned care based on how long perms (8–12 weeks) and color (4–12 weeks) last.
  • 10% off when you book on Naver and pay with Naver Pay, plus an extra 5% off for a photo review.
  • Clear pricing based on hair length and service time.
