From men's perms
to volume straightening and straightening + setting

At RIAHN Hair Hwagok Station, a regular perm is ₩80,000 Basic, ₩100,000 Design and ₩120,000 Premium (specialty perm), and a root perm is ₩50,000. Setting and digital perms start at ₩100,000 (Basic), and straightening + setting at ₩150,000 (Basic).

Straightening is ₩120,000 Basic (natural), ₩140,000 for volume straightening and ₩160,000 Premium. For men, perms start at ₩70,000 (Basic), men's straightening (natural) is ₩100,000 and men's volume straightening ₩120,000. Your designer consults with you first, then styles to suit the look you want. To change your color as well, see our hair color page.

Recommended for

  • Anyone who wants sleek hair with straightening or volume straightening
  • Anyone who wants natural waves with a setting or digital perm
  • Men who want a perm or straightening in our men's section
  • Anyone who wants crown volume with a root perm

FAQ

Perm FAQ

How much is volume straightening?
Volume straightening is ₩140,000, or ₩126,000 when booked on Naver and paid with Naver Pay. Men's volume straightening is ₩120,000 (₩108,000 with Naver Booking + Pay).
How much is a men's perm?
Men's perms are ₩70,000 Basic, ₩90,000 Design and ₩110,000 Premium (specialty perm), or ₩63,000, ₩81,000 and ₩99,000 when booked on Naver and paid with Naver Pay.
How long does a perm last?
We plan your next visit around a perm lasting 8–12 weeks, and recommend using home-care products.
