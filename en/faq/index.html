FAQ

Common questions about booking, parking, hours, prices, discounts, aftercare and booking a designer by name.

Home/FAQ
How do I book?
You can book your preferred date and time in real time on Naver Booking. You can also book or ask by phone (+82-2-6082-3383), Instagram DM or Naver chat inquiry.
Is parking available?
Parking support is 2 hours for services, 1 hour for cuts and 1 hour 30 minutes for down cuts. The building's tower parking is paid (₩1,500 for the first 30 minutes, ₩500 per additional 10 minutes, up to ₩50,000), and SUVs, Ray models and large vehicles cannot be parked. When it is full, please use Hwagok 3-1 Public Parking (40 Hwagok-ro 15-gil, Gangseo-gu, Seoul), 300 m from the salon, ₩600 per hour.
How much parking support do you offer?
We offer 2 hours of parking for services, 1 hour for cuts and 1 hour 30 minutes for down cuts.
What are your hours?
We are open daily 10:00–20:00. Please check days off on Naver Booking or call us (+82-2-6082-3383).
Where is the salon?
Unit 201, 2F, 135 Hwagok-ro, Gangseo-gu, Seoul. We are 298 m from Exit 7 of Hwagok Station on Subway Line 5. Come out of Exit 7 and walk straight along the street to our building.
What are your prices?
Men's cut ₩19,000, women's cut ₩22,000, regular basic perm ₩80,000, volume straightening ₩140,000, full color ₩70,000 and healing head spa ₩55,000. The larger amount in the price list is the regular price. Prices can vary with the service and your hair condition, so we confirm the exact price at your consultation.
Do you offer discounts?
Booking on Naver and paying with Naver Pay gets you 10% off, and writing a photo review gets you an extra 5% off. The smaller amount under a price is the price with Naver Booking + Pay.
Is there a student discount?
A student cut is ₩17,000.
How should I care for my hair afterwards?
We plan your next visit around how long styles last — 8–12 weeks for perms and 4–12 weeks for color — and recommend using home-care products.
Can I book a specific designer?
Choose your preferred designer when you book. We recommend Designer Roha for build perms and head spa, Designer Yunhee for C-curl and S-curl perms, Director Soyu for repair straightening and black color removal, Head Director Sohee for layered perms and volumizing extensions, and CEO Beom for repair treatments and down perms.

Didn't find your answer? Call us during opening hours (daily 10:00–20:00).
