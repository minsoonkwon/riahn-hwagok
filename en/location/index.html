Finding the salon

We are 298 m from Exit 7 of Hwagok Station on Subway Line 5. Come out of Exit 7 and walk straight along the street to our building. Parking support is 2 hours for services, 1 hour for cuts and 1 hour 30 minutes for down cuts. The building's tower parking is paid (₩1,500 for the first 30 minutes, ₩500 per additional 10 minutes, up to ₩50,000), and SUVs, Ray models and large vehicles cannot be parked. When it is full, please use Hwagok 3-1 Public Parking (40 Hwagok-ro 15-gil, Gangseo-gu, Seoul), 300 m from the salon, ₩600 per hour.
