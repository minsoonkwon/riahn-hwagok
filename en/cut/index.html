At RIAHN Hair Hwagok Station, a men's cut is ₩19,000, a student cut ₩17,000 and a women's cut ₩22,000 (shampoo extra +₩3,000). Down perm + cut is ₩40,000, with +₩10,000 for a back down perm, and a scalp cut is ₩30,000.

We run a separate styling section just for men. Our designers specialize in layered cuts. Your designer consults with you first, then styles to suit the look you want. For volume or waves, see our perms & straightening page too.

Recommended for

  • Men who want a neat cut in our men's section
  • Anyone who wants a light layered cut with movement
  • Men whose sides and back stick out (down perm + cut)
  • Students looking for a student cut
